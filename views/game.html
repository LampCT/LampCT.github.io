Connect 4

How to play

Connect 4 is a game where two players take turns dropping their colored piece from the top into the board. The goal of the game is to connect 4 pieces in a straight line.

How it works

The board is represented as an array of 42 elements.
Defining a compass rose to move through the array.
Recursive functions use the compass rose to "drop" pieces and check for 4 in a row.
